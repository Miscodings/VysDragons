Plugin Flow:

Egg - Idle when not incubating, (being placed in any heat source such as fire or magma block), then it hatches and baby dragon is spawned (3 minecraft days to hatch). All dragons use heat sources, there are no elements.
Baby Dragon - Acts like a sidekick following the player around, until it grows to young (30 minecraft days) Keep the quirk animation.
Young dragon - Flies and breathe fire, (60 minecraft days) (this is different from the current dragon system because the young dragon can basically be treated like an adult instead of JUST the adult dragons)
Adult dragon - Flies and breathe fire (2 Players seats)
